## Plain-language summary (for the captain)
1. Line of Command is a desktop game you buy once on Steam for about $19.99. It has no subscription and nothing to buy inside the game.
2. You play the general. You breed and train a barracks of up to 20 musket-era officers. In battle, each officer chooses his own formation and fights on his own.
3. Every day the war general offers you a few assignments at places on a campaign map. You pick up to 3 to fight. Each one is a battle against the computer.
4. During a battle you can send one messenger, either by clicking at the right moment or by setting an order beforehand, to rally the men or bring up reserves.
5. Two fort missions, Hold the Fort and Last Stand, give young officers a tougher proving ground. Practice battles and replays are free and unlimited, but they don't improve anyone.
6. V1 has no player-vs-player and no online war. The big shared online war is the future direction, and V1 is built so it can be added later.
7. The plan is a playable test version by Fri 2027-01-22 and a finished, Steam-ready build by Fri 2027-02-12. The earliest public launch is around Mon 2027-03-01, after Steam's review and its two-week "coming soon" period.

## Outcome
A Steam player buys Line of Command once, breeds and trains officers, takes up to 3 daily war-general assignments on the campaign map, watches fair, repeatable auto-battles they can steer with one messenger, and sees their bloodline improve over weeks. The game gives no buyer any advantage.
**Product instance:** Line of Command V1, a single-player desktop game built in Godot 4.
**Deploy surface:** Steam desktop builds (`economy.json → business_model.platform = "Steam"`) for Windows, Linux (including Steam Deck) and macOS. The spec names no ship list. The three platforms are inferred from check B1, which requires the same results on all three. See Q1.

## In
- **Officers** (spec §1-6): 5 attributes, Wisdom, 6 skills, 4 hidden attributes, trait genetics with hidden-slot reveals, and an Officer Rating shown for display only.
- **Breeding** (§7, D5/D7/D10): wobble sd 4, soft cap at 80, inbreeding costs, breeding preview showing p10/p50/p90, carriers shown once any relative has shown the trait, a 20-slot roster, manual release, and pedigree ghosts.
- **Training and ranks** (§4, §8): Lieutenant to General, with point budgets from 10 to 20.
- **Battle** (§9-13, §15-16): 3 unit types, 4 formations (counters ×1.07/×0.94, D2), symmetric terrain (D6), 3 stances, and a pure deterministic `resolve_battle` that writes an event log. The battle viewer is built from that log and has narration.
- **Messenger** (§14, D1/D8): a live click during the first viewing (no rewind) plus an optional standing order. The first one to trigger wins. Rally stays at 12% and its effect is labelled on screen.
- **Campaign map with war-general assignments (requester addition, 2026-11-22):** the 3 daily ranked battles appear as assignments at locations on a campaign map, with a few options offered each day. Every assignment is solo against the computer. *Rules and data are pending the SD spec update* (AC-7).
- **Scenarios** (§21, D12): Hold the Fort (47%) and Last Stand (35%), open to Lieutenants and Captains. The first attempt each day is ranked. Later attempts that day are free replays.
- **Free, no-progress modes** (D4): Skirmish against the computer and scenario replays, both unlimited.
- **Daily limits** (D9/D11): 3 ranked battles, 1 breed, 6 training clicks (at most 3 per officer). They reset at 00:00 UTC and are the same for every player.
- **Base-game extras** (§18): an unlimited replay archive, replay controls (pause, 4×, scrubbing, a per-turn stat overlay), default uniforms, banners and portrait frames, and the house heraldry editor.
- **Steam:** ownership check and build depots. Balance numbers are loaded from `data/*.json` and never hard-coded.

## Out (things people will try to sneak back in)
- **PvP of any kind**, including asynchronous ghost battles and leaderboards ranked against other players.
- **The shared Foxhole-style online war** (pick a side, NPCs fill in): OUT of V1. It is the future direction (see the Future-proofing note).
- **Subscription, in-game currency, microtransactions, timer skips, extra daily battles or breeds**, or anything else on the never-sold list (D11).
- **Any DLC at V1**, including the replay studio (share links, GIF/MP4 export). DLC comes later and must respect the never-sold list.
- **Web or browser build, servers, accounts, and a Supabase/React carry-over.** Web Ops (Cloudflare) is **not needed** for V1.
- **Deferred design:** chain of command, attacker/defender sides on normal maps (D6), ageing and retirement, banking unused battles, extra scenarios, and a scenario generator.
- **Mobile and console builds.**

**Future-proofing note (a constraint, not built in V1):** V1 must not block the online war. That means:
- (a) `resolve_battle` stays pure and deterministic, with no clock, file IO or scene tree (§15.1), so a server can later run it unchanged.
- (b) The campaign map, locations and assignment generation are data-driven (JSON) and seeded.
- (c) The officer, army and assignment records reserve a `side`/`faction_id` field, defaulting to null, and the save format carries a version number.
- (d) No networking, matchmaking, account or server code is written in V1.
